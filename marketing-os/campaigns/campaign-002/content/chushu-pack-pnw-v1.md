# 处暑 CHUSHU · Pacific Northwest Seasonal Pack #001
### 8月23日 – 9月6日 ｜ Aug 23 – Sep 6, 2026 ｜ Seattle metro

*What's peaking, what's ending, what to put on the menu, and what to post.*

---

**Two things up front / 先说两件事**

1. **The price column is blank on purpose.** Your supplier's price is the only one worth anything — mine would be a made-up number. Fill it once and the sheet works all fortnight.
2. **Verify at your own supplier.** Western Washington and Eastern Washington run two to three weeks apart, and Uwajimaya, H Mart, 99 Ranch, Viet-Wah and the farmers markets each stock differently. Cross out whatever doesn't match. This is a starting point, not a verdict.

处暑 means "the heat stops here." In this region that lands almost exactly right — late August is the last cheap week for summer produce and the first week of fall. **The move this fortnight: buy the summer stuff while it's at its floor, test the fall stuff in small quantities.**

**Also worth a look:** blackberries are everywhere right now, including free on the roadside — Himalayan blackberry is a regional joke and a real ingredient. Walla Walla sweet onions are at the very end of their season. Chanterelles usually start in September; ask your forager supplier if it's been wet.

**The buying call this fortnight:**
- **Load up:** corn, tomatoes, zucchini, winter melon. These are your margin.
- **Last call:** luffa, water spinach, edamame. Two more weeks, then gone or expensive.
- **Test small:** prune plums, early apples, Asian pears. This is your "only right now" story — and it's the only thing on this list a competitor can't copy next month, because next month it won't be true.

## 2 · Eight menu lines you can run this fortnight

Written so a line cook can execute and a customer can understand. Price them yourself.

| # | Dish | 中文 | Why it works now |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Winter melon & dried shrimp soup | 虾米冬瓜汤 | Highest-margin soup on the sheet. Winter melon is at its cheapest and it stretches anything. |
| 2 | Corn & egg stir-fry | 玉米炒蛋 | Two cheap ingredients, 4 minutes, sells to kids and to nostalgia. |
| 3 | Garlic water spinach | 蒜蓉空心菜 | Ending soon — running it as a special creates real urgency instead of fake urgency. |
| 4 | Yardlong beans, dry-fried | 干煸豆角 | Travels well. Best takeout vegetable on this list. |
| 5 | Bitter melon with black bean & beef | 豉汁苦瓜牛肉 | Your regulars order this; your new customers don't. Worth a line of menu copy explaining it. |
| 6 | Luffa with glass noodles | 丝瓜粉丝煲 | Last two weeks. Cheap now, gone by 白露. |
| 7 | Cold tomato with sugar | 糖拍番茄 | Zero cook time, near-zero cost, plates in 30 seconds. Free-appetizer material. |
| 8 | Asian pear & rock sugar dessert | 冰糖炖梨 | 处暑吃梨 is a real tradition and it makes a two-sentence story. Prep ahead, reheat to order. |

**How to run these:** 1–7 for volume, 5 with a line of explanation, 8 as the memory-maker. Items 3 and 6 are the ones to promote hard — they genuinely disappear in two weeks, so the urgency is true.

## 3 · Three posts you can publish today

Change the shop name and post. English for Instagram/Yelp/Google, Chinese for WeChat groups.

**(A) The "ending soon" post**

> EN: Water spinach and luffa are in their last two weeks. After that they're gone until next summer. On the specials board through Sept 6 — 蒜蓉空心菜 and 丝瓜粉丝煲.
>
> 中: 空心菜和丝瓜就剩最后两周了，过了这阵子要等明年。特色菜单上到 9 月 6 号：蒜蓉空心菜、丝瓜粉丝煲。

**(B) The "first of the season" post**

> EN: First Washington apples and Asian pears of the year came in this week. 处暑 — the old Chinese calendar says the heat breaks today. Feels about right. Pear and rock sugar dessert is back on.
>
> 中: 今年第一批华州苹果和梨到了。今天处暑，老话说暑气到头 —— 今年还真挺准。冰糖炖梨重新上了。

**(C) The "what we bought" post**

> EN: What's actually good this week: corn, tomatoes, zucchini, winter melon. All at their best price of the year, all on the menu. We buy what's cheap and good, not what's on a fixed list — that's why the specials change.
>
> 中: 这周真正好的：玉米、番茄、西葫芦、冬瓜，都是一年里最便宜最好的时候，都上了菜单。我们是看什么当季好就买什么，不是照着固定单子进货 —— 所以特色菜每两周会换。

> **Note on what is deliberately not in these posts:** no health claims. No 润肺, no 降火, no detox, no immunity. Those statements create liability you'd be passing to your own customers, and diners stopped believing them years ago. "It's in season, it's cheap, it's good, and it's gone in two weeks" outperforms them anyway.
